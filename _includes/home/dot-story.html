{% comment %} Homepage opening: one field of coloured dots that changes from shape to shape as you scroll (portrait, warbler, moment, landscape), with one line of text per shape: beside the shape, or at the top for a full-width shape (`place: top`). Every text is there in English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese. The dots are drawn by dot-morph.js from the shapes in dot-shapes.js; the lines come from `story` in _data/home.yml. The first line is the page title (the signed name, see signature.svg). Under each line after the title, a short line pointing to a page (`go`, story-go.html); the two are wrapped in .dot-step__line so they move and centre as one. Lines that move on their own (the title, and lines with `enter` / `leave` in the data) sit in the sticky layer with the dots, not in their scrolling step, so the browser holds them level while you scroll; line-moves.js only slides them in and out (data-pin = the page they belong to). Their step stays, empty, so the scroll is just as long. {% endcomment %} {%- assign story = site.data.home.story %} {%- assign title = story.first %}
