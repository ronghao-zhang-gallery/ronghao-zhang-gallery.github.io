{%- comment -%} The URL of one bird photo, nothing else (use inside a capture). From the image repo bird- (photo_hosts in _config.yml); a photo not imported there yet gives its local thumbnail in local-photos/ (local preview only). Params: photo (file name), date ("YYYY-MM-DD"), variant ("thumb", default, or "large": large only in full-screen views), local (true: always the local thumbnail, for fallbacks). Not "size": include.size is Liquid's count of the params (it gave ".../bird-2026/3/…"). {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign ps_variant = include.variant | default: "thumb" -%} {%- assign ps_year = include.date | slice: 0, 4 -%} {%- assign ps_repo = "bird-" | append: ps_year -%} {%- assign ps_host = site.photo_hosts[ps_repo] -%} {%- if ps_host and include.local != true -%} {{ ps_host }}/{{ ps_variant }}/{{ include.photo }} {%- else -%} {{ site.photo_local | append: "/" | append: ps_year | append: "/thumb/" | append: include.photo | relative_url }} {%- endif -%}
