{% comment %} Avians gallery (user, 2026-10-26: "below the bird cards, do a gallery of all photos"): every photo, newest first, as 3:2 thumbnail tiles, three to a row. Similar photos are merged into one stack (user, 2026-10-26): the same bird, on the same day, at the same place (one outing). A stack shows its cover (`cover: true` in avians.yml, else its newest photo) with card edges behind it and a count badge; single photos are plain tiles. Hovering a tile shows the bird's name and date · place (and the count). Clicking a tile opens the full-screen viewer (avians/viewer.html, photo-viewer.js) at its cover; the viewer steps through every photo one by one. Each photo is a hidden .photo-item inside its tile, carrying the viewer's data (large copy, EXIF) and its caption in all three languages. Tiles carry their continent, so the continent bar filters them (places-map.js), and their species, place, date, count and search text for the sidebar (avians/gallery-sidebar.html, gallery-filter.js), which searches, filters and sorts them. Each tile's id is "photo-" + its newest photo's name, the target of the bird cards' photos. Params: visits (the rows with a photo, sorted newest first), places. {% endcomment %}
